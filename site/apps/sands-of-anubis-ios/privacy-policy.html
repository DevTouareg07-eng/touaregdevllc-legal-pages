Sands of Anubis iOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

About this policy

This policy covers Sands of Anubis for iPhone and iPad, distributed through the Apple App Store and developed by Hasan Azman. The game does not require an account, and the developer does not operate a server that stores game profiles or progress.

The game does not ask you to provide your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location. If you contact support, the information you include in your message will be used to respond to you.

Information stored on your device

Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. This can include level progress, rewards, language selection, and sound or music settings. The game does not send this saved gameplay information to the developer. Resetting game progress, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app can remove it.

Advertising and Google services

The iOS app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show interstitial ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy choices where required. AdMob may process information such as an IP address, device or advertising identifiers where permitted, diagnostics and performance information, and information about ads shown or interactions with the app or ads. Google uses information under its own policies to provide and measure advertising and operate its services. See Google's iOS data disclosure, Google's Privacy Policy, and Google's advertising policies.

Where available, you can reopen Google's privacy options form from Privacy Choices in the game's Settings. Your choices may affect the ads available to you. Ads may also be limited or unavailable depending on connectivity and ad inventory.

Apple App Tracking Transparency

The app may ask for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt to access the device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for tracking as Apple defines it. This permission is optional. You can review or change it in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking. This setting controls Apple's tracking authorization for this app; other processing by Google is described in Google's policies above. Learn more about App Tracking Transparency.

Sharing, transfers, and retention

The developer does not receive the gameplay progress stored locally by the game. Google may process information through its advertising services and service providers, including in countries outside your own, under Google's policies and applicable safeguards. The developer does not control Google's retention practices; Google's privacy policy and account controls explain its available retention and deletion options.

Locally stored game progress remains on your device until you reset it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. The developer does not maintain a game account or server-side profile to delete.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can manage available advertising consent choices in the game's Privacy Choices settings and manage tracking authorization in iOS Settings. For information Google handles through its services, see Google's privacy controls. To ask the developer about information you provided directly or to make a privacy request, contact us using the email below. Your rights depend on the laws that apply to you.

Links and other services

If you open an external link, the destination service may process information under its own privacy policy. Apple, Google, hosting providers, and network operators may process technical information as part of providing their services.

Children's privacy

Sands of Anubis is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising services are provided by Google and are subject to the privacy choices and policies described above.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The date above identifies the current version. Changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the game, email contact@touaregdev.com.
