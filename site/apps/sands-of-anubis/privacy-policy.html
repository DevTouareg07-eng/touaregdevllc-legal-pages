Sands of Anubis

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Sands of Anubis handles information when you play the game. Sands of Anubis is developed by Hasan Azman.

The current version of the game does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, or other personal information.

Information stored on your device

The game stores gameplay progress and preferences locally on your device. This can include level progress, rewards, language selection, and sound or music settings. This information is used only to make the game remember your choices and is not sent to us by the game.

Uninstalling the game, clearing its storage, or using the in-game reset option can remove this locally stored information.

Advertising and third-party services

The mobile version uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to request interstitial ads after level results and some reward events. AdMob may process device identifiers, coarse device or network information, diagnostics, and ad interaction information to deliver, measure, limit, and prevent fraud in advertising. Google may use consent choices to determine whether ads are personalized. The game does not give rewards for accepting tracking.

AdMob's processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Where required, the game shows Google's consent choices before requesting ads. You can reopen available privacy choices from the in-game Settings panel. Advertising may be unavailable when consent is not granted or an ad has no fill.

The game also stores advertising pacing and consent-related state locally on your device. If you follow an external link, such as a link to these legal pages, that website may process information under its own privacy policy. Apple, Google, GitHub, hosting providers, and your network operator may also process technical information as part of delivering their services.

Children’s privacy

Sands of Anubis is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through a third-party advertising or store service, please contact that service and use the project contact below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The “Last updated” date above identifies the current version. Continuing to use the game after an update means the revised policy applies to your use of the updated game.

Contact

For questions about privacy or the game, use the project’s GitHub issue tracker.
